DIGITAL WEB DESIGN

Make every visit clear and useful.

A good website helps people find information, understand an organization, and take the next step with confidence.

Principles of a helpful website

  • Clarity: Use meaningful headings and concise content so visitors understand the page.
  • Consistency: Keep navigation, colors, spacing, and typography familiar across pages.
  • Accessibility: Provide descriptive alternative text, readable contrast, and keyboard-friendly controls.
  • Responsiveness: Design layouts that adapt to phones, tablets, laptops, and larger screens.
